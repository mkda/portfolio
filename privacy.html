Privacy and cookies

Last updated 25 November 2026

This is a personal portfolio. It sells nothing and has no accounts or forms. Below is everything it collects, and how to change your mind.

Who is responsible

Mikael Dell'Anna, Zurich, Switzerland. Contact: mikael@dellanna.pm.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web server, GitHub processes your IP address and request details to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. I don't receive or see these logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Fonts and scripts are served from this site itself, so opening a page doesn't contact Google Fonts or any other third party.

Analytics, only if you agree

If you click "Accept analytics", the site loads Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Ltd and Google LLC. If you decline or do nothing, it isn't loaded at all.

  • What it measures: pages viewed, the site you came from, rough location (country or city), device and browser type, and time on the page.
  • What it doesn't do: Google Analytics 4 doesn't store your IP address, and advertising features and Google signals are switched off.
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_<id> tell visits apart. They last up to 13 months.
  • Retention: event data is kept for 2 months, then deleted.
  • Transfers: Google may process data in the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US and Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection).

You can change your choice at any time with . Declining removes the analytics cookies from this site. The site asks again after a year.

Stored in your browser

Two small entries in your browser's local storage, which never leave your device: your consent choice, and your light or dark theme if you changed it.

Email

If you write to me, I use your message and address only to reply.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted, by email. Analytics data is anonymous to me, so I usually can't link it to a person. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or to the data protection authority where you live.
