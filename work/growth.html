Growth that finance can check

2021 to 2026 · Sunrise, then On

Growth earns trust when it is proved twice: once in the funnel, and once in the business case that paid for it.

Problem
A telecom eShop with drop-offs hidden in its checkout funnel, and engineering budgets that had to prove their return.
Role
At Sunrise, launched a new product portfolio and the rebranded eShop. Later, at On, built the three-year business case for extending RFID (radio-frequency identification) to 130 stores.
Result
Online sales grew 150% year on year in 2022 (2.5 times the 2021 figure), with growth continuing in 2023.

How it works

In the funnel

At Sunrise, every test had a KPI (key performance indicator) and a tracking plan. A/B tests, which compare two versions with real customers, ran across subscriptions, prepaid, internet and devices, through checkout, ID checks, activation, upgrades and renewals. Session recordings and customer feedback explained the why behind the numbers.

In the business case

Engineering investments come with a net present value case over months or years: what future savings are worth today, the break-even year, yearly profitability, and value drivers finance can check, such as loss prevention, recalls and compliance. At On, this approach shaped the business cases for the RFID rollout, described in Connecting physical products to data.

Decisions that mattered

Use finance's discount rate

The discount rate is the return the company expects from its investments, so it comes from finance. With their number in it, the case is one they can check line by line.

Speak the language of the P&L

The P&L (profit and loss statement) is where finance sees the result. Operating savings, such as fewer manual interventions, improve EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation) straight away. Capitalised engineering shows up later as depreciation. Saying so up front shows how the investment will look in the reported numbers.

Name the assumptions that matter

A few assumptions, such as adoption speed or the size of a prevented loss, move the result far more than the rest. Each case shows a conservative version beside the expected one, and what will be measured to see early which way it is going.

What carries over

Any team that has to prove value twice, to its users and to finance. Find where people drop out, test the fix with real users, and show finance when the investment pays back. That applies to a checkout, the onboarding of a trading app, or an engineering investment waiting for budget.
