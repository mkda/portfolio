Putting AI to work, with control built in

2024 to today · On and independent builds

Most AI pilots stall between the demo and daily use. The answer sits around the model: what it may decide, what code decides, how errors get caught, and what each good result costs.

Problem
Customs documents at On had to be checked against approved sources, and counterfeit checks depended on QR photos taken at any angle and quality.
Role
At On, delivered a RAG system for customs document review and worked on counterfeit QR image recognition. Outside client work, designs and runs AI products end to end.
Result
Quality gates that flag incorrect customs documents. In the studio, automated checks caught 173 issues on one episode before a person reviewed it.

How it works

Models interpret; software holds the permissions. Automated checks run before any person looks, and people decide at a few gates where judgment matters. Every model is tested on the company's own data, and when evidence is weak the system waits for more.

In production at On

  • Customs document review. A RAG system (retrieval-augmented generation: the model answers only from approved sources) built on validated Looker reports, with quality gates that flag incorrect documents.
  • Counterfeit checks. Image recognition that reads authenticity QR codes from photos sent in by customs offices, at any angle and quality.

Outside client work, the same approach runs an AI animation studio and a decision-support prototype. Both are on the Projects page.

Lessons from running AI in production

Three lessons from the studio, and how each carries over to retail operations, telecom, customer service and internal processes. The studio playbook article has all seven, with the measured results behind them.

Three lessons from the studio and how each applies in an organisation
LessonApplying it elsewhere
Machines check first. Automated staging, continuity and lip-sync checks run before anyone reviews a cut.Check every order, ticket or document automatically, so people focus on the exceptions.
Gates only where judgment matters. Two approvals: before money is spent, and before release.Keep approvals for decisions on spend, customer impact and compliance, and let routine steps flow.
Pay per usable result. Compare price divided by pass rate.Compare AI vendors on cost per successful task.

Where it fits in your company

Customer service replies, invoice and contract processing, store and warehouse exceptions. Agents draft, rules check, and people approve the cases that need judgment.
