01 · Case study

Physical-digital · Retail data · 2024–2026

Following every shoe from the factory to the shelf

Global performance sportswear brand · 100+ own stores · 5 developers, 3 vendors, CHF 250K+ yearly budget

The problem as I framed it

The tags were already on the products. The value wasn't. Data stopped at the warehouse door, so stores couldn't see what arrived, what sold or what went missing, and every exception became a manual ticket. This wasn't a hardware problem. The scans had to become trusted, real-time data that other systems could act on.

Options and the call I made

We needed serial numbers for every item. Buying a licensed serialisation tool was the safe choice. Instead I proposed a lean in-house serial-number MVP on top of the Digital Twin platform that already connected product, ERP and retail systems. It kept the data in one place and cost less to run, with $200K+ a year in projected savings.

Making the case

Scaling to 130 stores needed more than a pilot that worked. I built the 3-year business case and ROI model around three sources of value: loss prevention, faster and narrower product recalls, and readiness for the EU Digital Product Passport.

What was hard

Exceptions. As more stores went live, edge cases from 100+ locations kept arriving as manual work. We turned scans into event-driven data and set up a global support framework, which cut manual interventions by 70%.

What I'd do differently

Design the support model with the first pilot stores, not after the rollout had started. Operations scales differently from technology.

  • >99.9% item traceability
  • −70% manual interventions across 100+ locations
  • 500+ FTE days a year saved by the support framework (projected)
  • $200K+ a year saved by the in-house MVP (projected)
  • 130 stores covered by the 3-year business case

02 · Case study

E-commerce · Platform · 2021–2024

Rebuilding a high-volume sales channel while it kept selling

Swiss telecom operator · nationwide eShop and sales portal · ~20 engineers

The problem as I framed it

Three things had to happen at once: a new product portfolio, a new brand and a new platform. The monolith behind the sales portal and order flow slowed every release. The real risk was losing sales during the change, not the technology itself.

Options and the call I made

A big-bang rewrite would have frozen the channel for months. We moved the sales portal and order flow to microservices step by step over about 12 months, so the shop kept trading and every release could still ship.

How we found the growth

After launching the new portfolio and the rebranded eShop, I used funnel analytics to find where customers left. We removed those drop-offs one by one and measured each change, instead of shipping one large redesign.

What I'd do differently

Instrument the funnel before the migration starts. Our clean baseline came late, which made the early wins harder to prove.

  • 150% year-on-year online sales growth in 2022
  • Continued strong growth in 2023
  • ~12 months to migrate the portal and order flow, with no sales freeze

04 · AI builds · 2026

Products I build and run myself

Here I'm the PM, the architect and the operator, so I can show everything.

Live

LittleLeap Studios: an AI-run animation studio

Children's cartoons retold from public-domain tales. Agents built with Claude, Gemini and Seedance write, draw, animate, voice, check and publish each episode. A person approves the story, any new characters and the final cut.

  • 6 episodes, each in 6 audio languages
  • ~18 Shorts and 3 scheduled livestreams a day
  • Two web games and an iOS app in review, built from the same characters

Machines check first

Automated staging and continuity checks took one episode from 173 issues to zero before any human review. A heavier human review loop, by contrast, ran to 84 versions with no better result, so I rolled it back.

Pay per usable result

Comparing image providers on price divided by pass rate, not price per call, cut the cost of each usable drawing by a third ($0.12 vs $0.18) with the same model and quality.

Test on your own content

Every model is tested on the studio's own characters before it's adopted. Two video models passed 0 of 6 test clips between them; the one now on trial passed 3 of 3 at a lower cost per clip than the current one.

Prototype

Multi-agent market research and risk

Python system with separate research, validation and risk agents, using exchange data and a time-series forecasting model. Execution is off by default and runs only after validation and risk checks pass.

Guardrails first: trading stays switched off until validation and risk agents both pass.

iOS physics puzzle game

48 levels in SwiftUI and SpriteKit, taken from concept to pre-release, with a custom level-design tool to build and tune them.
