Physical-digital · Retail data

Following every product from factory to store shelf

Global performance sportswear brand · 2024–2026

Challenge

Products carried RFID tags, but the data stopped at the warehouse. Stores could not see what arrived, what sold or what went missing, and every exception needed a person to fix it.

What I did

  • Led the RFID technology workstream from factory shipment to 100+ stores, with 5 developers, 3 vendors and a CHF 250K+ yearly budget.
  • Shaped the Digital Twin platform that links product, ERP and retail systems.
  • Built the 3-year business case for 130 stores, putting numbers on loss prevention, product recalls and Digital Product Passport compliance.
  • Delivered an in-house serial-number MVP instead of a licensed tool.
  • >99.9% item traceability
  • 40M+ scans a year turned into event-driven omnichannel data
  • −70% manual interventions across 100+ locations
  • 500+ FTE days a year saved by the support framework (projected)
  • $200K+ a year saved by the serial-number MVP (projected)
E-commerce · Platform

Rebuilding a high-volume sales channel while it kept selling

Swiss telecom operator · 2021–2024

Challenge

The sales portal and order flow ran on a monolith that slowed every release, at the same time as a new product portfolio and a new brand had to go live online.

What I did

  • Steered a ~12-month migration of the sales portal and order flow to microservices with a team of ~20 engineers.
  • Launched the new product portfolio and the rebranded eShop.
  • Used funnel analytics to find where customers dropped off, then removed those steps one by one.
  • 150% year-on-year online sales growth in 2022
  • Strong growth continued through 2023
  • Faster iteration on a platform that scales

03 · Independent builds · 2026

What I build on my own time

Hands-on products where I am the PM, the architect and the operator. They keep my AI and engineering judgment current.

Live

AI-run animation studio

An AI-assisted production workflow with Claude, Gemini and Seedance that writes, animates, voices, checks and publishes children's cartoons. Automated quality checks run first, and a person approves the story, new characters and the final cut.

04 · Approach

How I work

  1. Start from evidence

    Customer research, funnel data and operations numbers come before opinions. If we can't measure it, we define how we will.

  2. Make the case in money

    A roadmap item earns its place through a business case: value, cost, risk and the point where we stop.

  3. Speak engineering

    My developer background means I can debate an API contract or an event schema, and spot scope that only looks small.

  4. Ship small, check early

    MVPs, automated checks and clear approval gates. Quality comes from good checks, not more process.

  5. Lead across lines

    Engineers, vendors, stores, finance and leadership pulling in one direction, with budgets and people managed openly.
