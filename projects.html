Projects

Independent products, designed, built and run end to end, as a one-person team. The AI case shows how the same approach works inside a company: Putting AI to work.

Live

LittleLeap Studios

An AI-run animation studio for children's stories retold from public-domain tales. Agents built with Claude, Gemini and Seedance write, draw, animate, voice and publish each episode. A person approves the story, new characters and the final cut.

Every script, video and livestream is checked against content rules for ages 3 to 7. An automated reviewer flags anything frightening, violent or off-brand before the approval gate, and a person signs off each episode before release.

6 episodes in 6 languages · 3 scheduled livestreams a day · $11–67 of AI spend per finished episode (inference only, before human review time)

Prototype

JEV: AI decision support with guardrails

A research platform for market data, tested on digital-asset markets, that combines signals, cited research and forecasts from TimesFM, a time-series model from Google, in one traceable workflow. AI interprets the evidence. Code owns permissions, sizing, accounting and orders.

Holds back whenever evidence is missing, stale or contradictory · private build, walkthrough on request
