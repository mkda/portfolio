Design system

The tokens and components every page on this site is built from. Change a token in styles.css and every page follows. Switch the theme in the top bar to check both modes.

Colour

Semantic names, two themes. Accent is for links, numbers and the one primary action per screen.

Type

Geist for reading, Geist Mono for labels and numbers. Seven sizes, fluid at the top end.

--fs-2xl Page title
--fs-xl Section title
--fs-lg Block title
--fs-md Card title and ledes
--fs-base Body text for paragraphs
--fs-sm Captions and meta

--fs-xs Label

Space and shape

A 4px scale. Sections use --section-y, which grows from 48px on phones to 96px on wide screens. Radii: 8, 12, 16 and pill.
